01Can I buy Nordform today?

Nordform is presented here as a visual and product concept. Request availability to receive verified specifications, a launch date and a purchase link when ready.

02Will themes support TYPO3 13 and 14 LTS?

That is the planned compatibility target. Exact supported versions will be listed on each released product and checked against its changelog.

03Can agencies use a theme for multiple clients?

The proposed Agency licence is intended for repeat client work. Final usage terms will be published before sales open.

No. CMSMotive is an independent product brand. TYPO3 is a registered trademark of the TYPO3 Association.

Use the contact form and share your email, agency or company, TYPO3 version and the licence you are interested in. We reply within one business day.
